# AURA Medic Mesh Pro Notes

AURA Basic is the free ARGUS layer for emergency medical context. It does not
diagnose, triage professionally or replace medical care.

Future AURA Pro may include:

- professional triage workflows;
- ambulance coordination;
- hospital and bed availability;
- medical stock status;
- transfer coordination;
- sanitary command dashboards;
- ARGUS Routing Intelligence integration;
- ARGUS Fenix Twin integration;
- audit trails;
- institutional roles;
- secure interoperability.

These features are intentionally not implemented in the current task.
